About Food Joint

Fresh, hot food without the fuss

Food Joint is located right in the heart of Bhangagarh on G.S. Road—situated near Nemcare Hospital and opposite Angelika Higher Secondary School. We started with one straightforward goal: serve hot, filling, and tasty meals to people who need good food at honest prices.

A lot of our regulars are doctors, nurses, hospital staff, families visiting loved ones at Nemcare, and students from nearby institutes. When you're busy or worried, you don't need fancy tablecloths or inflated prices—you need a clean steel plate with piping hot rice, homestyle dal, fresh sabji, or a quick roll made fresh right in front of you.

Local Note: Most full meals cost between ₹70 to ₹190. You can dine in at our clean counter seating or take quick parcel packages for hospital wards and offices.

Everything is cooked fresh daily in our open kitchen. From our ₹70 Veg Thali to our popular Egg Chicken Rolls and hot chowmein noodles, we keep things simple, hygienic, and affordable.
